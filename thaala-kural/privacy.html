Privacy Policy — தாளக் குறள் · Thaala Kural

Central Institute of Classical Tamil · செம்மொழித் தமிழாய்வு மத்திய நிறுவனம்

This app collects no personal data, and it never uses the internet. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code of any kind. The Android app does not even hold the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Nothing a child does in the game leaves the device.

In short: the whole game — the 1,330 couplets, their metre charts, the meanings, the drum sounds and the Tamil font — is packaged inside the app. It works completely offline, on the first launch and every launch after.

What is stored, and where

The app remembers a few things so a child can carry on where they left off. All of it is written to the app's own private storage on the device, under the keys tk.settings.v1 and tk.progress.v1. It never leaves the device, it is not shared with any other app, and it is deleted when the app is uninstalled. It can also be erased from inside the app (அமைப்பு → முன்னேற்றத்தை அழி).

Settingslevel, tempo, guide drums, sound and vibration switches, English subtitles, left-hand pads, high contrast, big text, and the sound-timing offset measured on the device
Progressstars and best score per kural, how many rounds were played, the last kural played, and the day-streak counter with the date of the last round

None of this is an identifier. There is no name, no age, no device ID, no advertising ID, no account and no profile. The app never asks a child to type anything.

Reading aloud

The கேள் (listen) button speaks a couplet using the text-to-speech engine already installed on the device. The app passes that engine the text of the couplet and nothing else. Which engine that is, and whether it processes speech on the device or on a server, is decided by the device and its settings, not by this app — see the Android text-to-speech settings to check or change it. On a device without a Tamil voice the button simply says so.

Sounds and vibration

The drum sounds are small audio files bundled in the app and played on the device. Vibration on each tap is optional and can be switched off in the app's settings; it uses the VIBRATE permission and nothing else.

Permissions

VIBRATEto pulse the device on each drum tap, only while the vibration switch is on

That is the only permission. The app requests no access to the internet, contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, notifications or call data.

Links

The app contains no links that leave it. There are no external websites, no social media, no store links and no other apps opened from inside the game.

Children

The app is made for children and is suitable for any age. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children. There are no advertisements, no in-app purchases, no chat, no user-generated content and no way to contact other people through the app. Parents and teachers do not need to create anything or supervise any setting for the app to be safe to use.

The web version

The same game also runs in a web browser. There, progress is kept in the browser's local storage for that site, and a service worker caches the game's files so it keeps working offline. The web version makes no requests other than loading its own files from the site that hosts it, and sets no cookies.

Changes

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or sends, this page will be updated before that version is published, and the date below will change.
